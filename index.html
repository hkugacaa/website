Preface

Dear Members, UGAers and friends,

Welcome to the new website of the HKUGAC Alumni Association (HKUGAC AA)! This site has been designed to better serve the growing community of HKUGAC & HKUGAPS graduates and foster stronger connections among our alumni.

The HKUGAC AA was established in 2012 with the mission of supporting our alma maters and providing opportunities for our members to stay engaged with HKUGAC, HKUGAPS, and one another. Over the past 12 years, we have grown to over 500 active members living around the world.

Whether you graduated last year or years ago, this website is your gateway to the HKUGAC AA community. Here you will find information about our latest initiatives, upcoming events, and ways to get involved.

Our alumni community is a valuable and vibrant network. We hope this new website will help you rediscover the spirit of HKUGAC & HKUGAPS, renew old friendships, and unlock new possibilities. We look forward to welcoming you and working together to advance the legacy of our beloved alma maters.

Sincerely,
The HKUGAC Alumni Association Executive Committee

HKUGAC Alumni Association Executive Committee 2024-2026

2024 – 2025 Year Report and Financial Report

2025 – 2026 Year Plan and Indicative Budget Plan

Date: 13 September 2025

We, as the Executive Committee of HKUGAC Alumni Association of 2024-2026, are pleased to present our 2024-2025 year report and financial report, and also 2025-2026 year plan and indicative budget plan to our fellow members.

2024 – 2025 Year Report

This past year, the Alumni Association (AA) has focused on strengthening connections within our alumni community and fostering meaningful engagement between alumni and current students. We're proud to report on a year filled with successful initiatives and renewed vibrancy.

Organisational Development

We're thrilled to report on the significant growth and strengthening of our team this past year. Our Executive Committee (ExCo) has become more comprehensive, now comprising 8 dedicated members. This expanded leadership has allowed us to achieve more impactful results. Furthermore, we successfully recruited 5 additional sub-committee members (Jamie Lee, Justin Sae-ung, Lincoln Siu, Marco Law, Eunice Wong) throughout the year. This growth in our volunteer base has greatly enhanced our capacity to plan and execute a wider range of activities for our alumni community.

Alumni Manager

Continuing our established practice, the Alumni Association successfully conducted the Alumni Manager Election. This process ensures that alumni voices are represented in the governance of our alma mater, maintaining a vital link between the past and present of our college.

Alumni-Alumni Connection

We prioritized creating diverse opportunities for alumni to reconnect and build new relationships.

  • Alumni Day (October): Over 50 alumni returned to the college for a fun-filled day of activities, including exercise, board games, school tours, and shared snacks. It was wonderful to see so many familiar faces enjoying their time back on campus.
  • Open Day (December): Our booth at the College Open Day was a great success, with over 50 alumni visiting to connect with us. We distributed stickers and souvenirs, and we truly hope to see everyone again next year.
  • BBQ Gathering and 3x3 Basketball Competition (March): This joint event brought together over 80 teachers, students, and alumni for a fantastic day of friendly competition and delicious food. It was a true testament to the strong community spirit within our college.

Alumni-Current Student Connection

A key focus this year was bridging the gap between our alumni and current students, providing valuable support and guidance to the next generation.

  • Sharing Platform - WhatsApp Community: We developed a dedicated WhatsApp platform to facilitate direct communication between alumni and our Secondary 6 students. This platform provides a valuable avenue for alumni to share insights and experiences, particularly in assisting students with their JUPAS choices.
  • Pastoral Lesson Sharing: Approximately 15 alumni generously volunteered their time to share their university subject choice experiences with our Secondary 6 students during pastoral lessons. Their real-world perspectives were invaluable for students navigating crucial academic decisions.
  • Reminder Notes and Blessings for S6: As our Secondary 6 students prepared for graduation and the DSE result release day, the AA prepared special reminder notes to share our blessings and care, offering encouragement during this significant time.
  • Tutorial Network: To further support current students academically, we established an online platform for a Tutorial Network. This initiative connects parents with available alumni who are willing to provide tutoring, creating a valuable resource for enhanced learning.

Administration and Communication

We also made significant strides in enhancing our administrative infrastructure and communication channels.

  • AA Website Established: We successfully launched the official Alumni Association website, providing a central hub for information, news, and resources for our alumni community.
  • WhatsApp Community for Communication: A dedicated WhatsApp Community was created as a primary communication channel, enabling us to share the most updated news and announcements with alumni efficiently and effectively.

Looking Ahead

This year has been a testament to the strong and vibrant community that is our alumni association. We've seen increased engagement, valuable connections forged, and meaningful support provided to both alumni and current students. We look forward to building on this momentum in the coming year, continuing to foster a strong and supportive alumni network.

2025 – 2026 Year Plan

For the upcoming year, the Alumni Association (AA) will continue to build on the successes of 2024-25. Our core objectives remain clear: (1) to strengthen the engagement between the Alumni Association and current students in collaboration with the School; (2) to cultivate a vibrant culture within the Alumni Association and further connect our diverse alumni community; and (3) to establish a robust and sustainable internal framework for the Association.

1. Signature Annual Events: Fostering Connection and Tradition

We plan to keep the following activities from last year as signature annual events, building on their success and embedding them into the fabric of our alumni calendar. These events aim to bring together alumni, current students, teachers, and parents, celebrating our shared community.

  • October: Alumni Day x Board Game Competition — Building on the positive feedback from last year, our annual Alumni Day will once again invite alumni back to campus for a day of reconnection and fun. We'll introduce a Board Game Competition to add a new element of friendly challenge and interaction.
  • November: Open Day – Celebrating the School's 20th Anniversary — We will actively participate in the School's Open Day, particularly as it marks the significant milestone of the School's 20th Anniversary. This will be a prime opportunity for alumni to celebrate with the school community and reconnect.
  • December: Volleyball Fun Day x Christmas Celebration — To foster camaraderie across the entire school community, we will host a Volleyball Fun Day, inviting teachers, parents, and students to participate alongside alumni. This will be coupled with a joyful Christmas Celebration to end the year on a festive note.
  • February: Lunch Gathering x 3x3 Basketball Competition — Our successful 3x3 Basketball Competition will return, expanding to include teachers, parents, and students for even broader participation. This energetic event will be complemented by a relaxed Lunch Gathering, providing an informal setting for networking and catching up.

2. Strengthening Internal Affairs and Sustainability

To ensure the long-term effectiveness and resilience of the Alumni Association, we will focus on enhancing our internal framework.

  • Formalizing and Sustaining the Alumni Manager (AM) Election — Building on our existing practice and the recent constitutional amendments, we will continue to formalize and conduct the AM election procedure for both the College and the Primary School. Our roadmap includes: securing acknowledgment from HKUGA EF for HKUGAC AA as the representative of both Schools' alumni, followed by their request for the Schools' IMC to commence the AM appointment; receiving official requests from the Schools' IMC to proceed with the AM election; and continuing the AM election procedure in strict accordance with the AA Constitution. Our goal is to ensure the smooth conduct of the AM election for the College by mid-2026 and to further liaise with the Primary School to expedite their AM election process.
  • Financial Arrangements — We will actively explore diverse financial strategies to achieve a sustainable financial position for the Alumni Association. As an active contributor to the School, we aim to collaborate closely with the school administration on resource management, seeking potential support for the arrangements of school-related events.
  • Recruitment of Sub-Committees — Building on the successful recruitment of 5 sub-committee members last year, we will continue our proactive efforts to recruit more sub-committee members. Expanding our volunteer base is crucial for effectively managing and executing the growing number of initiatives and events planned.
  • Establishment of Class Representatives Network — To enhance communication and engagement across all graduating cohorts, we aim to establish a comprehensive network of Class Representatives. These representatives will serve as key liaisons between their respective classes and the Alumni Association, helping to disseminate information and gather feedback.

3. Deepening Engagement Between Alumni and Students

Building on the success of last year's initiatives, we are committed to providing even more avenues for meaningful interaction between alumni and current students.

  • Career Mapping Programme — We will continue to organize and expand the Career Mapping Programme, connecting alumni from various graduation years to serve as mentors and share invaluable career advice with students. We will work closely with the school's career team to organize more sharing opportunities, ensuring current students gain comprehensive insights into their future career and study choices.
  • Enhancing the Tutorial Network — Following its successful pilot scheme last year, we will focus on enhancing the Tutorial Network. Our goal is to find innovative ways to make this platform even more useful to a wider range of students, potentially extending its reach to younger age groups within the school.

4. Cultivating Alumni Culture and Community Connection

We will continue our efforts to foster a strong sense of community and pride among our alumni.

  • Alumni Perks — We will coordinate more workshops and networking events, serving as a dynamic platform for our alumni to connect, share expertise, and build professional and personal relationships within the community.

Application for Visiting Alumni

Types of Visitors

Former Students

Individuals who have attended HKUGAC for one year and subsequently departed, while their peers have not yet graduated.

Alumni

Individuals who have successfully graduated from HKUGAC, or have left HKUGAC but belong to a cohort that has completed their studies.

Visitors Must:

  • Have studied at HKUGA College for at least 1 year.
  • Have an appointment with someone at the college.
  • Register via the Google Form at least 2 working days in advance.
  • Provide the following information: full name; contact details (phone number and email address); HKID number (first 5 digits, including the first letter); purpose of visit; contact person (a teacher you would like to meet — please alert them before submitting the form); and estimated duration on campus.

General Rules for Visitors:

  • Former students may visit only during lunchtime (13:00 – 14:00) and after school (15:40 – 18:00).
  • Visitors are allowed in the canteen, amphitheater, corridor, and other communal areas, unless supervised by a staff member.
  • Alumni are encouraged to visit teachers in the afternoon (after 13:00).
  • For visits outside of these times, please register via the Google Form at least 3 working days in advance for approval.
  • Visitors must not enter classrooms or specialist areas, such as the science lab or special rooms.
  • Warnings will be issued to visitors who violate the rules above.
  • Serious violations may result in suspension of visiting privileges.
  • The AA and the school reserve the right to deny entry to any visitor.

Reporting Procedures:

  • Upon arrival, show your approved registration form.
  • Obtain a visitor badge from the security station at the car park. This badge must be worn while on campus.
  • Wear appropriate clothing and avoid excessive accessories. HKUGAC uniform items are not allowed.
  • Go to the staff room and meet the teachers who have been contacted. Please locate the teacher you are visiting and inform them of your presence.
  • Return your visitor badge at the reception and sign out before leaving.

Special Program:

The registration arrangements will differ for the following events, so please check the AA and School website 2–3 weeks before the event dates. Visitors must register using a different link.

  • Christmas Program (registration deadline: 18/12, 12:00 nn)
  • Sports Day
  • Swimming Gala

General Expectations for Visitors:

  • Respect school policies and procedures. Familiarise yourself with and follow the school's current rules, regulations, and visitor protocols.
  • Maintain appropriate behaviour. Act as a positive role model for current students; avoid inappropriate language, unsuitable attire, or unprofessional conduct during your visit.
  • Prioritise student learning. Be mindful not to disrupt classes or school activities. Do not enter classrooms without obtaining permission from the school.
  • Protect student privacy. Do not take photographs or videos of students without explicit permission from the school administration as well as the students and their parents.
  • Avoid unsupervised interactions. Do not attempt to meet with or interact with students one-on-one without a school staff member present.
  • Offer constructive feedback. If you have suggestions for improvement, please provide them directly and respectfully to the appropriate school authorities rather than to the students.
  • Maintain confidentiality. Refrain from sharing sensitive information about the school, staff, or students that you may have access to as an alumnus.
